Questions

Good to know

How do I set up an engagement?
Easiest

Option 1: use the free AI assistant

It works for you. Just tell it what you want, and your engagement is ready in about 2 minutes.

Option 2: set it up yourself

Work directly in the organizer console and choose every setting your own way.

Do participants pay or install anything?

No. Participants never pay, never install an app and never set a password. They open your link and type their mobile number.

Who gives the rewards?

You do, the organizer. We provide the software only and never collect or handle reward money.

How is the organizer verified?

Through the link or the 6-digit code we send to your e-mail. There is no password to remember.

What happens to the data?

We delete all personal data, including the mobile numbers and names of all participants, from all our systems within 24 hours. We keep only the details of the organizer, host and winners, and these are deleted automatically after 365 days. A game that never starts is treated the same way: its guest data is deleted 20 hours after the time it was meant to end. Corporate Tambola is designed to follow India’s DPDP Act, the EU and UK GDPR and US privacy laws. Read the privacy summary.

Can families and guests from other countries take part?

Yes. Participants can be employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors, ex-employees: anyone you value, in any country.

How it works

From payment to rewards, in five steps.

One organizer sets it up in about 15 minutes. Participants need only a phone and a link: no app, no SMS, no password. They are your employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors, ex-employees: anyone your organization values, in any country.

  1. 1

    Create Engagement

    Sign in with your e-mail and build your engagement: how many participants, rewards, brand breaks. Building and publishing are free. Share your link with everyone, then pay once, per engagement, before it starts. No yearly plan.

  2. 2

    Add participants

    Paste or upload mobile numbers from any country. We tidy the formats and remove repeats. Choose 1 ticket or 2 tickets each.

  3. 3

    Share the link

    Send one link by WhatsApp, e-mail or your intranet, or show a QR code on screen.

  4. 4

    Everyone takes part on their phone

    Participants open the link, type their mobile number and get their own ticket. A clear voice calls every number.

  5. 5

    Rewards and report

    1st to Nth Full House are checked by the software. You hand over the rewards yourself and download the report.

Optional

Play it inside your Google Meet, Zoom or Teams call

Nothing to install in the meeting app. Everyone talks in the call as usual; the game runs alongside.

  1. Start your Google Meet, Zoom or Teams call as usual. Everyone talks there, as always.
  2. Paste the guest link in the meeting chat (or send it on WhatsApp). Each person opens it on their own phone and gets their ticket. The phone shows only the number just called.
  3. Share your screen in the meeting and show the number screen (console, step 6: “Open the number screen”). It shows the number just called and the prizes, never names or numbers.
  4. Choose who calls the numbers before the start (step 2): the software at a steady speed, or you press Next number yourself while you talk. You cannot change this once the game has started.
  5. Brand breaks and your speech play on every phone. The software finds the winners and shows them on the number screen, so you can announce them in the call.
