Questions

Good to know

How do I set up an engagement?
Easiest

Option 1: use the free AI assistant

It works for you. Just tell it what you want, and your engagement is ready in about 2 minutes.

Option 2: set it up yourself

Work directly in the organizer console and choose every setting your own way.

Do participants pay or install anything?

No. Participants never pay, never install an app and never set a password. They open your link and type their mobile number.

Who gives the rewards?

You do, the organizer. We provide the software only and never collect or handle reward money.

How is the organizer verified?

Through the link or the 6-digit code we send to your work email. There is no password to remember.

What happens to the data?

We delete all personal data, including the mobile numbers and names of all participants, from all our systems within 24 hours. We keep only the details of the organizer, host and winners, and these are deleted automatically after 365 days. We also delete all backups within 7 days. A game that never starts is treated the same way: its guest data is deleted 23 hours after the time it was meant to end. Corporate Tambola is designed to follow India’s DPDP Act, the EU and UK GDPR and US privacy laws. Read the privacy summary.

Can families and guests from other countries take part?

Yes. Participants can be employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors, ex-employees: anyone you value, in any country.

How it works

From payment to rewards, in five steps.

One organizer sets it up in about 15 minutes. Participants need only a phone and a link: no app, no SMS, no password. They are your employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors, ex-employees: anyone your organization values, in any country.

  1. 1

    Create Engagement

    Sign in with your work email and build your engagement: how many participants, rewards, brand breaks. Building and publishing are free. Share your link with everyone, then pay once, per engagement, before it starts. No yearly plan.

  2. 2

    Add participants

    Paste or upload mobile numbers from any country. We tidy the formats and remove repeats. Choose 1 ticket or 2 tickets each.

  3. 3

    Share the link

    Send one link by WhatsApp, email or your intranet, or show a QR code on screen.

  4. 4

    Everyone takes part on their phone

    Participants open the link, type their mobile number and get their own ticket. A clear voice calls every number.

  5. 5

    Rewards and report

    1st to Nth Full House are checked by the software. You hand over the rewards yourself and download the report.

Included with every engagement

An AI assistant for the organizer or host. Absolutely free.

You get a private work link for your own AI assistant. It does the work for you: set up the engagement, load your participant list, schedule it, run the console, pause and resume, check the results and prepare the report. It never changes the rules and cannot touch who wins.

Pricing

Simple pricing, per engagement, no commitment.

Packs are sold as "Up to N participants", per engagement. No yearly plan, no extra fees. We do not give refunds. If you cancel a paid game before it starts, your payment stays as credit for your next game for 365 days. Unused credit lapses at the end of day 365. The organizer keeps the credit in their account.
