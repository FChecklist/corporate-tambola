Questions

Good to know

Do participants pay or install anything?

No. Participants never pay, never install an app and never set a password. They open your link and type their mobile number.

Who gives the rewards?

You do, the organizer. We provide the software only and never collect or handle reward money.

How is the organizer verified?

Only by a 6-digit code sent to your work email. There is no password to remember.

What happens to personal data?

We keep a name, a mobile number, a department if given, and who invited the participant, nothing else. Tickets and phone details are deleted 24 hours after the engagement ends. Names and numbers are kept for up to 365 days after they last took part so the organizer can reuse the list, then deleted. Anyone can ask us not to keep their details. Read the privacy summary.

Can families and guests from other countries take part?

Yes. Participants can be employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors, ex-employees: anyone you value, in any country.

How it works

From payment to rewards, in five steps.

One organizer sets it up in about 15 minutes. Participants need only a phone and a link: no app, no SMS, no password. They are your employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors, ex-employees: anyone your organization values, in any country.

  1. 1

    Create Engagement

    Sign in with your work email and build your engagement: how many participants, rewards, brand breaks. Building is free. Pay once, per engagement, only when you publish it. No yearly plan.

  2. 2

    Add participants

    Paste or upload mobile numbers from any country. We tidy the formats and remove repeats. Choose 1 ticket or 2 tickets each.

  3. 3

    Share the link

    Send one link by WhatsApp, email or your intranet, or show a QR code on screen.

  4. 4

    Everyone takes part on their phone

    Participants open the link, type their mobile number and get their own ticket. A clear Indian-English voice calls every number, twice.

  5. 5

    Rewards and report

    1st to Nth Full House are checked by the software. You hand over the rewards yourself and download the report.

Included with every engagement

An AI assistant for the organizer or host. Absolutely free.

You get a private work link for your own AI assistant. It does the work for you: set up the engagement, load your participant list, schedule it, run the console, pause and resume, check the results and prepare the report. It never changes the rules and cannot touch who wins.
