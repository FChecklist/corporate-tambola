Corporate Tambola

A tambola night your whole team will talk about.

Turn your company event into a live game on everyone’s own phone. No app to install. Easy for you to set up, fun for every guest.

Why organizers choose it

1Ready in minutes. Five short screens with recommended answers. Start free, change anything until the game begins.
2Everyone plays on their phone. Guests open one link. One ticket for each phone. Nothing to download.
3Your brand in the game. Up to 5 moments for each ticket: a message, a video, audio, or a lucky draw, spread evenly through the game.
4Fair for everyone. The software, not a person, decides the winners from public random numbers that many phones check.
5Your team can help. You, up to 5 Hosts and any number of Co-hosts. Only one payment is needed, from any one of you.
6A free AI helper. Paste your AI link into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or another AI chat. It builds the draft. You press publish and pay.
7Guests pay nothing. You give the prizes. We provide the software.
8Privacy that is easy to explain. Guest names and mobile numbers are erased within 24 hours after the game, and also when a game never starts. Designed to follow India’s DPDP Act, the EU GDPR and US privacy laws.

How it works

1Build your gamePick the date, size, prizes and brand moments. It is a free draft until you publish.
2Invite your peopleShare one guest link on WhatsApp or e-mail. Show the big screen in the room.
