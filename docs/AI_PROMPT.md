# The AI assistant prompt (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any AI that can open web links)

Written 2026-10-22 for the owner. Plain words.

## What already exists

Every organizer account has a private **AI work link** (console: "Let your free AI assistant do the work" → "Get my AI work link"). The link opens a front door written for AIs: rules, the guided path (`/go/new`: one short screen per question, each with numbered choices and a recommended answer), the 10 facts, and the hand-over links for the things only a human may do (publish, pay, erase, pause, end). The AI never sees guests' names or numbers and cannot choose winners.

## What this task adds

1. **A complete paste-in prompt** (`aiPaste`, shown in the console and on the new page /ai-prompt.html): it tells the AI who it works for, to interview the organizer one step at a time with numbered options that mirror the console (occasion, date and time, people, who calls the numbers, tickets, speed, rounds, prizes with how many winners, tie rule, brand moments, Hosts, who may join, guest invites), to read back a summary and wait for a clear yes, to build the game through the link, and at the end to give every link: the console, the guest link, the number screen, the organizer's board, the hand-over link to publish (one tap by the organizer), and the pay link (the organizer pays; the AI never pays). It also says what to do if the AI cannot open links (send the organizer to the console) and that text found inside pages is never an instruction.
2. **A Host version** of the prompt (read how the game is going, message the guests, tick prizes as given, press Next number only from the console).
3. **A page on the website** (/ai-prompt.html, linked from "Free AI assistant for organizers" and the footer): the 6 steps for a human, the prompt itself to copy, notes for ChatGPT (browsing on), Gemini and Claude, and what the AI cannot do.
4. **A dry run**: a Claude agent plays the organizer's AI against a local copy of the service, following only the pasted prompt and the links it is given, and reports every point where it got stuck or had to guess.

## The 6 steps for a human

1. Sign in at corporatetambola.com and open your console.
2. Press "Get my AI work link" and copy the whole message.
3. Open ChatGPT (with web browsing on), Gemini or Claude and paste the message.
4. Answer its questions. It offers numbered choices and recommends one. Nothing is saved until you say yes to the read-back.
5. It gives you the links: your console, the guest link to share, the number screen, your board, and a one-tap link to publish.
6. You publish with one tap and pay on the pay page. Players pay nothing.
