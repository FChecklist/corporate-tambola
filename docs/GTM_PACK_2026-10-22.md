## 3. Objections and the short answer

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| Is this gambling? | No. Guests pay nothing. You give the prizes. It is a party game run by software. (Legal position: https://corporatetambola.com/legal-position.html) |
| We do housie with paper tickets already | Paper takes 2 people and arguments over claims. Here every claim is checked, winners agreed by the phones, and you get the report. |
| Our people are not tech-savvy | A link, name, mobile number. No app, no sign-up. The ticket is big; tap the number, press Claim. |
| What about data? | Names and numbers are deleted 24 hours after the game. Winners kept 365 days so the prize can be handed over. |
| Can our MD speak in between? | Yes: brand moments - a welcome speech, a video, a message between numbers, a lucky draw. |
| Can we play on Zoom / Teams? | Yes, optional: share the number screen in the meeting; guests play on their phones. |
| Price? | On the pricing page. One payment per game, by size of the crowd. The demo is free. |
