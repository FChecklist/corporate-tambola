# The ten steps, read as a first-time organizer (25 October 2026)

Method: a new organizer signs in on the real site, the first game is made for them, and every step is opened and read in Chrome (and, for the code, dumped in an automated browser in auto and manual choices). For each step: what a person sees, whether the order of the options is logical, and what was changed.

| Step | What it asks | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Date and time | Occasion, day, time | "Who calls the numbers" was here, far from the gap it controls | Moved to step 2 |
| 2 Number of participants | Size, who calls the numbers, gap, tickets, games in a row, who can join, employees only, what a person must give, guests bringing people | Gap was shown for manual; guest-invite sentence stayed for employees only; "rounds" unexplained | Order is now: size, **who calls the numbers**, **gap (only for auto)**, tickets, games in a row (rounds explained), who can join, employees only, what a person must give, guests bringing people (hidden with its sentence for employees only) |
| 3 Prizes | Which houses, how many winners, what prize | Logical | none |
| 4 Brand moments | Company name, colour, logo, videos, lucky draw | Logical | none |
| 5 Hosts and Co-hosts | Who helps | In manual mode it did not say a Host presses the numbers | A line is shown when manual is chosen |
| 6 Invite and share | People list, links, play yourself | Logical | none |
| 7 Check and publish | Read-back, publish | Wording mentioned an AI helper | Removed. The read-back says "a Host presses for each number" for manual |
| 8 Pay | Price and payment | Shows "Publish first" until published; correct | none |
| 9 Game day | Run, pause, message | Logical | none |
| 10 After the game | Report, erase data | "Delete all participant data now" showed only a plain question | Before the start and during the game it now warns what is lost (tickets, seats, invitations, report without guests) |

Also changed from this review: the e-mail address is shown at the top right of the console; the assistant strip and AI wording are not shown in the console; "Create a new game" opens this console directly.

Still to confirm by a person: that the Quick setup link is gone from every page a customer sees (the old link now opens this console).
