## Positive findings worth keeping

- Schema parity: the code's `SCHEMA` constant and the 18 migration files build identical schemas, and the raw live export differs from them only by `telemetry` (created by the Pages Function) and the pending 0018.
- Live `outbox` rows (9) keep no body (`length(body) = 0`), as the privacy design says.
- Header parity: every `_headers` promise (CSP, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy and others) matches the live responses on all 83 files.
- A whole 30-phone game finishes in 5 to 10 s of real time; two real Chrome phones finish a game in about 35 s with `page.clock`; winners were identical everywhere in every run (one false alarm from my own check, a late joiner with a 12-number log, fixed in the checker, not a product bug).
- Games end early when every prize is awarded (logs of 72 to 89 numbers): intended (`endedByPrizes`).
