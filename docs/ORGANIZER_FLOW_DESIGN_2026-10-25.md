# Who is the game for? The logical flow of step 2 (decided 25 October 2026)

**Rule:** no change to what the server stores. Every choice below is only a clearer way to set the settings that already exist: *who can join* (list or anyone), *only my employees*, *what a person must give* (mobile, employee ID, either, both), and *guests may invite*. The screen asks one question first, then shows only the questions that still make sense.

## Step 2, in this order
1. **How many people at most?** One choice from the size list (10 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 750 / 1000 / 2500 / 5000 / 10000). A different number is not offered next to it; a small link "My crowd is a different size" opens a number box for the rare case.
2. **Who calls the numbers?** *The software, at a chosen speed (auto)* or *A Host presses a button for each number (manual)*.
3. **The gap between two numbers** (9 or 12 seconds): shown only when the software calls the numbers. With manual, a line says the Host decides the pace.
4. **Tickets for each person:** 1 or 2.
5. **How many full games in this event:** 1 (most events) up to 5. This is about games played one after the other, with new tickets and numbers each time. It is not the same as the 1 or 2 tickets each person holds.
6. **Who is this game for?** (one choice, below)
7. **What must a person give to get in?** (only where it applies, below)

## The one question: "Who is this game for?"
| # | Choice | Settings behind it | Then ask | Then show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Only my employees** (the list I upload) | list + employees only | What must an employee give: *Employee ID* · *Mobile number* · *Employee ID or mobile* · *Employee ID and mobile* | Step 6: upload the staff list. Nobody can invite anyone |
| 2 | **My employees, plus their friends and family** | list + employees + guests may invite | Employees give ID and/or mobile (as in 1). Invited people give their mobile number | Step 6: staff list. Each employee can invite up to 4 people by WhatsApp |
| 3 | **A list of people I choose** (customers, vendors, members, dealers) | list, not employees only | Nothing: mobile number only (an employee ID does not apply) | Step 6: upload the list. Anyone else who opens the link waits for approval |
| 4 | **A list of people I choose, plus the people they invite** | list + guests may invite | Nothing: mobile number only | Step 6: upload the list. Each person can invite up to 4 |
| 5 | **Anyone with the link** | anyone | Nothing: mobile number only | Step 6: share the common link. No list needed, no waiting room |
| 6 | **Anyone with the link, my employees confirm with their employee ID** | anyone + employee ID rule | *Employee ID* · *Employee ID and mobile*. Others give their mobile number | Step 6: share the link and upload the staff list |

## What changes in the other steps because of the choice
- **Step 6 (Invite and share):** choices 1 to 4 and 6 lead with "Add your people" and the file upload; choice 5 leads with the common link only. The waiting-room explanation appears only for list games (1 to 4).
- **Step 7 (Check and publish):** the read-back names the choice in plain words, for example "For: my employees and their friends and family; employees give their employee ID".
- **Step 5 (Hosts):** if manual was chosen, a line says a Host (or you) presses each number.
- **Step 10:** unchanged except the erase warning before or during the game.
- **Help (i) texts** are rewritten to describe the six choices, what each does and does not do, why, how, and what happens if the common link is shared with a list-only game (the person is told to wait for approval).

## What does not change
The size list and prices, prizes, brand moments, the server, the stored settings, the reports and the audit. Existing games keep working: their stored settings are shown as the nearest choice.
