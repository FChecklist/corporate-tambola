# Note for the owner: the crossed-out "regular" price

Decision (owner, 2026-10-05): keep the crossed-out price and the 50% offer on the pricing page. The page now says **Festive offer: 50% off** and shows the crossed price as the **regular price after the festive offer**. The price charged, and the price in the search data (INR 1,999 to 24,999 before GST), is the offer price.

Why this needs your attention later (a lawyer should confirm the details for each country):

* India: Consumer Protection Act 2019 s2(28) (misleading advertisement) and the Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023. A "was" price should be a price you really intend to charge after the offer.
* United States: FTC Act s5 and the FTC guidance on reference prices. The regular price should be a price at which the product is genuinely offered, or will be.
* EU and UK: the Omnibus Directive and the UK DMCC Act 2024 require the reference price to be the lowest price in the previous 30 days, unless the offer is a genuine introductory price.

What to do:

1. When the owner has decided how long the festive offer runs, decide with the lawyer whether and how to show it. No end date is written anywhere in the code, pages, e-mails or documents; the owner gives it later.
2. When it ends, either charge the regular price shown, or remove the crossed price.
3. Review this note with your lawyer before you advertise outside India.
