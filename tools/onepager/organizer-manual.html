CORPORATE TAMBOLA · ORGANIZER'S ONE-PAGE MANUAL

Set up your game in 10 small steps.

Online housie for your closed group: employees and their families, dealers, customers, vendors. Every mistake on a screen explains itself and tells you what to do next, so you cannot get stuck.

WHAT YOU NEEDAn e-mail address and a phone with WhatsApp. About 15 minutes. Your players need only a phone and your link: no app, no password, no payment from them. Not sure yet? Start with a free demo game for up to 10 people (every screen says DEMO GAME).
1

Sign in

corporatetambola.com → Log in
  1. Type your e-mail. We send a 6-digit code; type it.
  2. No password, ever. The code is good for one sign-in.
  3. Press Start a new game, or Start a free demo game to try it first.
Bookmark your console: you come back to it for every step below.
2

Date and time

Console step 1
  1. Pick the occasion, the day and time (India time). Name the game if you like.
  2. The first number is called 2 minutes after the start time; players use those minutes to join.
  3. Choose who calls the numbers: the software at a steady speed, or a Host presses Next number for each one.
The calling mode cannot be changed once the game has started.
3

Number of players

Console step 2
  1. Pick the biggest crowd you expect (invited guests count too).
  2. Who can join: only people on your list, or anyone with the link.
  3. 1 or 2 tickets each, the speed (6 to 15 s a number), up to 5 rounds, and whether guests may invite others.
A demo game stays at 10 people until you make it a real game.
4

Prizes

Console step 3
  1. Tick the prizes: Early Five, lines, Four Corners, Star, Full House and more. Each says how to win.
  2. Set how many winners each prize has (1st, 2nd, 3rd…) and what the prize is.
  3. Choose the tie rule: people finishing on the same number take the next prizes one by one, or share that prize.
You hand the prizes over. We only find the winners.
5

Brand moments

Console step 4 (optional)
  1. An opening while players wait, up to 3 breaks during the game, a closing at the end.
  2. Each can be your video, YouTube link, slides or a speech; a break can also be a lucky draw.
  3. Add your logo and colour: they appear on every ticket.
Everything plays on every phone at the same moment.
6

Hosts and Co-hosts

Console step 5 (optional)
  1. Add up to 5 Hosts (their e-mail or mobile). Each gets a link and sets up once.
  2. A Host can run the game day, pause, end it, press Next number and pay. A Co-host helps with one game only.
  3. You can retire a Host at any time.
A Help Desk partner can prepare the game for you; only you publish and pay.
7

Invite and share

Console step 6
  1. Copy the guest link or send it on WhatsApp; or add people to My people (type, paste or upload) and send personal links.
  2. Show the number screen on a TV, or share it in Google Meet, Zoom or Teams (optional).
  3. My board (the full housie board) is for you alone. Do not share that window.
Players see only the number just called: they must stay alert, like real housie.
8

Check and publish

Console step 7 · free
  1. Read the summary. Confirm your e-mail and mobile number.
  2. Press Confirm and publish. Players can now open the link and get their tickets.
  3. A demo game is live at once, free. A real game still needs step 9 before it starts.
Publishing is free and can be done days ahead.
9

Pay

Console step 8 · once per game
  1. Pay before the start time; an unpaid game does not begin. A late payment starts it 2 minutes after paying.
  2. Pay yourself, or send a pay link to whoever pays in your company. A Host or Co-host may pay too.
  3. Your invoice arrives by e-mail. No yearly plan.
A demo game: press "Make this a real game", choose the size, pay. The game carries on.
10

Game day

Console step 9
  1. Open your console before the start time. That is what lets Pause and End the game reach every phone.
  2. Numbers come on their own, or you press Next number. You can Pause, message every phone, or End the game.
  3. The software finds every winner from the called numbers; nobody can cheat, not even you.
Prizes still open are listed on top; claimed ones are crossed out with "claimed".
11

After the game

Console step 10
  1. Each winner confirms their name and number on their own phone; you tick Prize given when you hand it over.
  2. Your report (PDF) is e-mailed: timings, numbers, prizes, winners, who invited whom.
  3. Players' names and numbers are erased within a day; winners' details stay 365 days for your records.
Press "Create a new game like this one" to reuse the whole setup.
!

If something goes wrong

You are never stuck
  • Every message says what happened, what to do, and takes you to the exact box. Nothing you typed is lost.
  • Internet dropped? Phones catch up on their own with the same numbers.
  • A player with two phones gets one ticket; the second phone is told politely.
  • Still stuck? WhatsApp +91 96503 97480 with the reference shown on the screen.
Golden rules: test with a free demo first · share the link early · open your console before the start.
