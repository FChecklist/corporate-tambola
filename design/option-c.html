How it works

Three steps. One unforgettable evening.

The organizer sets it up in minutes. Participants only need a phone and the engagement link.

01

Set up

Choose date, time, tickets per participant and your rewards. Add up to three brand breaks.

02

Share the link

Participants join with their mobile number and wait in a lobby. You admit who belongs.

03

Go live

Numbers are called with voice, participants cut their own tickets and claim rewards.

04

Report

A tidy engagement report for the organizer. Personal data is deleted after 24 hours.

Built for trust

Private by design

Participants see no one else

Results show ticket IDs only. Names and numbers stay with the organizer.

Brand breaks

Your video or a YouTube link, shown to everyone at once, up to three times.

Scale

From a team of 100 to 10,000 participants in one live engagement.

Pricing

One price per engagement

Pick the pack that fits your group. Participants never pay. Packs are sold as places: "Up to N participants".

Designed for DPDP, GDPR and US privacy laws. Reward rules vary by country; the organizer is responsible for their rewards.
